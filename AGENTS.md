# BITS.go — Engineering Guidelines

## Brand Philosophy
- **Build**: Turn business needs into dependable digital systems.
- **Innovate**: Employ modern web technologies, AI tools, and creative thinking.
- **Transform**: Automate manual workflows and streamline business operations.
- **Scale**: Architect technology for sustained growth.

## Core Rules
- Keep design premium, minimalist, and responsive.
- Respect brand identity (BITS.go / Bamietech IT Solutions).
- Ensure mobile accessibility and fast loading performance.
